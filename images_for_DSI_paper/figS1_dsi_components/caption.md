**Figure S1.** Decomposition of DSI and DSIr for the three illustrative windows of Fig. 1 (full history, post-1987 and recent, each ending in the last observed year) in each aggregated commercial group. The bar stacks the weighted base components (depletion slope 0.35, effort contrast 0.20, CPUE contrast 0.20, sample size 0.10, catch–effort coherence 0.10) on the 0–100 scale; the black tick is DSI after the outlier penalty; the diamond is DSIr, obtained by multiplying DSI by the robustness factors for missing-data coverage, regression influence, leave-one-out slope stability and fit–effort coherence. The anchovy full-history window starts in 1972. DSI rewards contrast and therefore scores the full history highly in the demersal group; DSIr discounts fragile windows.
