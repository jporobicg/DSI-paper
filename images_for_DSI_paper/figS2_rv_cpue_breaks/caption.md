**Figure S2.** Research-vessel (RV) survey CPUE by functional group (log scale), 1971–2023, with the breakpoints detected by the single-split F-test with binary segmentation (dashed; blue downward, orange upward; each line is drawn between the last year before the break and the first year after it). Grey bands mark years without a survey (1990 and 1992–2002); the Indian mackerel series ends in 2011. Six of the eight groups (anchovies, demersal mixed fish, large pelagic finfish, neritic tuna, sardines and scads) break downward after 1987.
