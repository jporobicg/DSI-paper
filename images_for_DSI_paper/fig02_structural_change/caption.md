**Figure 2.** Structural change in the Gulf of Thailand fishery series. (A) Ecosystem-scale research-vessel (RV) abundance index (mean of standardised log RV CPUE across groups), 1971–2023. Grey bands mark years without a survey (1990 and 1992–2002). Blue lines are the segment means before and after the 1987 break (dashed). The level shift is −1.55 standardised units (bootstrap 95% interval −1.81 to −1.29), and the F-statistic for the 1987 split exceeds its AR(1) null (p = 0.025). The dotted line marks a secondary upward break in 2016. (B) Breakpoints detected in 25 annual series: RV CPUE by group and the ecosystem index, effort by fleet and in total, total catch by group, and the demersal (DMF) share of the total catch. Each triangle marks the last year before a break and its direction (single-split F-test, α = 0.05, at least five years per segment, binary segmentation up to two breaks); all 45 breaks survive Benjamini–Hochberg control at 0.05. (C) Number of series breaking in each year. The dashed line is the 95th percentile of the largest same-year count under a null in which every series is an AR(1) process around its mean with no common break (2000 simulations). The observed 1987 cluster of eight series (black) exceeds this null (p = 0.0025). Breaks around 1977 are catch increases during fleet expansion; the 1987 cluster is a decline in abundance and effort; the 2015–2016 breaks coincide with the regulatory transition in the Thai fishery. The RV series themselves are shown in Fig. S2.
