**Figure 4.** The estimation window changes the assessment. Biomass relative to B_MSY for each functional group under seven estimation windows: the full history (start 1971), the post-1987 break, the recent window (2016), per-species windows set from research-vessel breakpoints, the Thai Department of Fisheries assessment starts, and two windows chosen by the DSIr screen (the optimum of each aggregated DOF group applied to its member species, and the optimum of each species' dominant fleet). (A) Terminal year of the fit (2023). (B) After a 20-year projection under status-quo effort (2043). Model structure is identical across windows; only the start years differ. (C) Estimated biomass trajectories for neritic tuna and sardines, the two groups most sensitive to the window; end markers identify the window. Fitting the full history forces present-day neritic tuna biomass to roughly 30 t against roughly 200 t under the post-1987 window, because a single set of stationary parameters must reconcile the high pre-1987 abundance with the depleted recent period. Neritic tuna shows a ridge between r and K in all windows and its absolute scale is weakly identified. Projections are deterministic point estimates without intervals.
